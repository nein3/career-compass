About Career Compass

Helping Grade 12 students make informed decisions about their future pathways.

Our Purpose

Career Compass is an online career guidance platform designed to support Grade 12 STEM students of Philippine Science High School – Caraga Region Campus in exploring opportunities after Senior High School.

The website provides information about college, employment, gap year opportunities, scholarships, and career preparation resources.

Who We Serve

This platform is created for Senior High School students who are preparing for their next step after graduation.

  • 🎓 Students planning for college
  • 💼 Students entering the workforce
  • 🌱 Students considering a gap year
  • 📚 Students looking for opportunities

Career Compass Objectives

Provide Guidance

Help students understand possible pathways and career choices.

Encourage Decision Making

Provide reliable information to help students make informed decisions.

Prepare Students

Develop confidence and readiness for future academic and career goals.
